About Me

More than just a student

Currently navigating my first year at NST RU, I see university as more than academics — it's a launchpad for discovery. I'm the kind of person who says yes to new experiences, dives headfirst into unfamiliar challenges, and finds joy in the process of learning itself.

Whether it's picking up a new chord progression on my guitar, pushing through a grueling badminton match, or finding inspiration in a completely unfamiliar city, I thrive on growth. I believe that the most meaningful things in life happen outside your comfort zone.

I'm not defined by one thing — I'm shaped by every interest I pursue, every person I meet, and every challenge I take on. My goal isn't perfection; it's to keep evolving, stay open-minded, and make every day count.
